# Code of conduct

Flare is for getting friends outside. We want working on it to feel the same: kind, plain and welcoming to everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Use plain words; explain jargon.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries. Never share someone's personal details without their consent.
- Take responsibility when you get it wrong, and fix it.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or threats, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Mocking anyone's disability, accent, looks or background.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Repeated disruption after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions), in project chats, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository and choose "conduct" in the title, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept confidential. We will reply within 7 days.

## What happens next

Maintainers may ask you to change or remove a contribution, warn you, or ban you from the project, in that order of seriousness, depending on what happened.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

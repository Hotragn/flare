## Screen notes

- **H1 Camera:** the viewfinder fills the content area with 18 px photo radius and four 34 px corner brackets; the shutter is 84 px (5 px ink ring, maple core). The menu is the only global navigation.
- **H2 Develop:** the print develops over 2.4 s (`--dur-develop`) while the hub drafts six words (lab: about 3.0 s). Words arrive one every 220 ms. If the hub can't be reached, a six-word field appears with a template placeholder. The FlameMeter below shows the host's unlit flame: "This is your flame. It grows as friends say yes."
- **H5, I1, I9, P4 FlameMeter:** the flame (124 px) sits on a ground shadow with a gold glow that widens with progress; the count line (22 px bold) and dots sit beside it. This is the screenshot moment.
- **I1 Invite:** the invite words are the page's h1. Under the place: "Met here N times" and the newest Spark when the group has history (BR-K-5).
- **I7 Compass:** the pointer is the brand pin-flame with its hole on the dial centre, rotated by bearing; three rings pull in as distance halves; facing within 15° lights the gold dot and a breathing glow.
- **I12 Go outside:** the golden-hour scene (same art as the DEV cover) and one line pointing outside; only a Close button.
- **O8 AI on this phone:** states the lab result honestly: photo AI doesn't fit in a phone browser yet; words come from the laptop in about 3 s.

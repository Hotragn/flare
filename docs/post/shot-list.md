# Field-test recording: shot list

For the founder's iPhone screen recording during the field test. Portrait, about 10 s per shot, 8 shots, about 90 s in all. Turn on Do Not Disturb first so no notifications from other apps show. Record the screen (Control Center, Screen Recording, long-press to turn the mic on only for shot 5).

| # | Moment | Phone | What to show | About |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The photo | Host | Open Flare from the Home Screen, take the photo of the spot, the print develops and six words appear | 10 s |
| 2 | Send it | Host | Pick how many, Send it, then the share sheet with the link going to the group | 10 s |
| 3 | The invite | Friend | Tap the link in Messages or Telegram, the invite opens with the photo and the six words, tap I'm in | 10 s |
| 4 | It's lit | Host | The count fills and the flame lights; if you can, show the friend's phone lighting at the same time (a second recording, cut together) | 10 s |
| 5 | Find them | Friend | Find them, the compass turns, the spoken direction ("Fuego, 120 meters, to your right") with sound on | 10 s |
| 6 | Arriving | Both | Phones down, people meet at the bench. Film this with a second phone's camera, not the screen | 10 s |
| 7 | A Spark | Friend | I'm here, leave a one-line Spark, Save it | 10 s |
| 8 | Telegram | Any | The group chat: the Flare card, the count edit after a tap, and the card gone after the end | 10 s |

If there's time, one extra: an Ember run, where not enough people say yes in time and it shrinks to "Smaller plan: 15 min at …".

Before posting: blur faces of anyone who didn't agree to be shown, and check that no full names, phone numbers or other chats are visible in the frame. Keep the files in docs/post/media/ under 10 MB each (iPhone: Photos, Edit, trim to the shot).

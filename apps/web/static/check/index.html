Device check

About 15 minutes per phone, all four phones at once. Do the steps in order. After each page, tap Send to laptop (or Copy results and paste in the Flare test chat).

  1. Capability page, 4 min

    Tap every button top to bottom. Allow motion, location and camera when asked. Do the Switch haptic and Audio cards twice: once with the silent switch off, once on.

    Open capability page

  2. Flame frame rate, 3 min

    Leave it alone for 10 s per state, then read the fps and p95 lines. Tap each state once.

    Open flame check

  3. Haptics, 2 min

    Tap each test and answer Felt it or Nothing honestly.

    Open haptics check

  4. Telegram, 3 min

    The laptop runs the test. When it says "waiting", one person sends /start@FairlaneFlareBot in "Flare test". Two people tap I'm in, one taps Not now, one opens the deep link.

  5. Compass, 3 min, outside

    Tap Start, allow both prompts, mark a spot, walk about 30 m, turn slowly in a circle and listen.

    Open compass check
